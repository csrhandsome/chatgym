# Signal Strength

This icon system treats coaching as a form of focused transmission. The visual center is a speech bubble built with the weight and confidence of training equipment, so the mark reads first as guidance and immediately after as strength. The composition should feel meticulously crafted, reduced to only the forms that carry meaning, and refined until the symbol survives the smallest app-grid sizes without losing intent.

Color is used as disciplined warmth rather than decoration. The background lives in a pale beige family that feels calm, tactile, and human. Soft sand and muted sage create atmosphere without noise, while a restrained terracotta ember signals intelligence, ignition, and the instant when a plan becomes action. Every relationship should look calibrated by hand, not sprayed on by default tooling.

The symbol language prefers rounded industrial geometry: dense enough to feel capable, soft enough to feel approachable. The speech bubble is the coach. The dumbbell is the work. The spark is the model turning input into direction. These parts must lock together with painstaking attention to spacing so the mark feels like one idea, not three unrelated references.

The final result should feel premium, contemporary, and purpose-built for a product that turns AI into practical physical progress. It must appear labored over with master-level restraint: crisp silhouette, generous padding, warm contrast, and no unnecessary detail. Craftsmanship is the product here as much as the icon itself.
